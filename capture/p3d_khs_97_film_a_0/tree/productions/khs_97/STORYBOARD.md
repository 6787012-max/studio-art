# khs_97 storyboard - short97 "or bli kli" (light without a vessel), phase B, film_a, 25.54 s (clip 24.54 + tail 1.0)

Idea: every light must have a vessel; the light is the soul. Light without a vessel scatters and loses its uniqueness. So the body, even when refined very much, stays dark compared to the soul. The soul always stays the thing that shines, and the body is the receptacle that holds it: it does not shrink it, it holds it and defines it.

Look: deep twilight indigo-plum sky fading to soft violet near the horizon; violet hills; sand-beige stone plaza; oak-wood plinth; a big clear soft-teal glass vessel; warm golden-amber light; cream accents. One central hero on a round plinth. Camera: slow push-ins, one small arc (max 40 deg in shot 3), a tilt up from the plaza to the glow, a final pull-back high. Objects only: no people, hands, arms, faces or silhouettes.

In-scene Hebrew (plaques from the vault, LBL_A to LBL_H only): shots 1 to 5 each carry one or two plaques.
