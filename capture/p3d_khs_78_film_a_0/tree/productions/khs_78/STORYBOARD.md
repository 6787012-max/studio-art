# STORYBOARD - khs_78 / film_a (vertical 1080x1920, 59.41 s)

Source: Rabbi Avshalom Schneider, lesson before Tisha b'Av 5785 (kolhalashon 41852449), 246.55-311.45 with 3 cuts. Voice mixed locally.
Idea (the Maharal): Israel being out of its place is an UNNATURAL state; a pledge is mine but held by someone else; things that belong
together cannot stay scattered - the very scattering proves they must gather; under the fourth kingdom -> sign of release, it must end.
So the exile ITSELF is proof of the redemption.
Central visual metaphor: a NAVY SPHERE that belongs in a GOLDEN SOCKET on a white pedestal ("its place"). It is held far away,
tied back by a taut gold thread (pledge), pressed down on a coiled SPRING by four heavy blocks. The more unnatural the pressure, the
more the spring stores; in the end the spring releases and the sphere flies home.
