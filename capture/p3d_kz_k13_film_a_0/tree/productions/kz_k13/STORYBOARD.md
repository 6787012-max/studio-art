# kz_k13 STORYBOARD - "the world BEGAN, so it has a goal" (Rabbi Avshalom Schneider, kol halashon lesson 42772336, parashat Bereishit)

Central visual metaphor: the CLOSED RING versus the OPEN ROAD. The philosophers' world is a ring track on which a small glowing
ball rolls round and round forever, going nowhere. "In the beginning" BREAKS the ring open at a bright START line: the ring
straightens into a road that runs forward to a far glowing GATE (the goal / the World to Come). This world is the road - a
corridor (prozdor) that leads to the hall. A small warm LANTERN (= the person / his soul, no figure) travels the road; side doors,
balloons and toys try to pull it sideways. At the end, sharp GLASSES keep the road and the gate in focus; without them the scene
blurs into a swirl. Finally a small amusement park turns out to be only a stop on the road (a work field), not home.

Characters: NONE in the whole film. The person = the lantern; the child = a tiny bed / small shoes only.
Palette (different from k12 sage/coral, k11 walnut/plum, k10 indigo): early-winter Bereishit morning -
soft slate blue #6B8CAE, snow cream #F7F3EA, terracotta #D9774B, pine green #4E7D6A, mustard #E9B64A (spice only),
ink outline #2F3440. Flat toon materials, clean bright backgrounds, never dark/black.
