# STORYBOARD - khs_76 / film_a "What is water, really?" (vertical, 65.61 s)

Lesson: Leshem, Hakdamot uShearim 1, ch.4 (Rabbi Avshalom Schneider). Idea: we never grasp the ESSENCE of anything - not a thing, not another person, not even ourselves - only how it shows itself outward. Water: I can play with all its phenomena (evaporates, liquid), but what IS water? Why: the inner point of each thing is how it came out of His will, and His will can never be grasped. The greatest scientist gives all phenomena and measurements, can explain HOW it works and build plans, buildings, engineering - but WHY is it like this, why this shape and not another - he does not know.

CENTRAL VISUAL METAPHOR: every object in a bright gallery has a small glowing GOLD SEED (its essence) sealed deep inside a frosted shell. Everything we can reach - light rays, measuring tools, a magnifier - touches only the outer shell and bounces off. In s04 thin golden threads rise from every seed up to a soft light above the frame (the will) that no tool reaches.
