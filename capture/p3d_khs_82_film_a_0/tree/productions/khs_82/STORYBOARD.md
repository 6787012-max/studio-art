# STORYBOARD - khs_82 "The Torah is already inside you" (vertical, 67.33 s)

Central metaphor: a clear GLASS SOUL-SPHERE on a pedestal that holds a COMPLETE golden lattice (nodes + lines = "all the Torah"). A thin golden thread of light ties its top to the bright light above (the soul is connected to Hashem). Frosted VEILS (concealments) close around it and dim the lattice. Learning = golden TILES that come from outside and re-light nodes that were ALREADY there - nothing new is added. A SMOOTH disc cannot take the gold at all (you cannot learn something for the first time); a disc with a faint IMPRINT (reshimu) drinks it in.
