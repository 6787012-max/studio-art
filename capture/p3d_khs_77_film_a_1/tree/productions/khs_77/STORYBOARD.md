# STORYBOARD - khs_77 / film_a (vertical 1080x1920, 58.78 s)

Source: Rabbi Avshalom Schneider, Daat Tevunot (Ramchal) lesson, 41733949, 2274.4-2348.5 with 3 cuts. Voice mixed locally.
Central visual metaphor: ONE GIANT PUZZLE. Everything in creation is a piece; the pieces lock together into one whole picture,
and at its centre a single golden crown (= "only one thing: Hashem is King"). A speeding-ticket toy scene is shown as a tiny
piece that zooms out into the huge picture. Hidden pieces stay veiled, yet their outline glows (never despair).
