Lesson idea (English paraphrase; the real voice is mixed locally; lesson for the Three Weeks): the Gra on the verse "the autumn has passed,
the rain is gone, the buds appear in the land" - two periods of the exile. AUTUMN = cold, it hurts the BODY: the first stage, decrees and
blows on the bodies; it hurt, but the MIND stayed whole - a Jew knew exactly who he is and who the attacker is, and the more blows, the more
the attacker is nothing. The second stage, "the RAIN": dripping rain disturbs the mind - like a tap dripping all night, it drives you mad.
That is the exile of the MIND: no more blows, no more suffering - but our mind is very confused, not firm, not clear: who are we? what is
our role? what is our worth?

Central metaphor: a GLASS LANTERN with a steady golden FLAME and a COMPASS NEEDLE that points straight up (= the clear Jewish mind),
standing inside a sturdy round STONE SHELTER (= the body). Stage 1: hail beats the shelter and cracks it, but inside the flame and needle
stay perfect. Stage 2: no hail, the shelter is whole and comfortable, but a slow DRIP keeps falling and the glass fills with mist; the
needle starts to spin.

Character: none (objects only). Location: a small round stone courtyard on a meadow at dusk-to-night, an autumn tree beside it
(different from khs_73 book/tiles and khs_74 workbench). Palette: soft lavender sky 0xd8cfe6 -> 0xf3eef6, pale stone floor 0xe2dbd0,
burgundy 0x9c3b47 (leaves, hail), mustard-gold 0xe8a92a (flame, needle), slate-blue 0x5d7290 (rain, drops, tap), mint 0x7fcfa8 (bud),
warm stone 0xc9b8a3 (shelter). Camera style: LOW ground-level tracking + a PUSH-IN THROUGH A CRACK into the shelter + a slow top-down
spin at the end (khs_73 orbited, khs_74 dollied laterally).
