# STORYBOARD - khs_79 / film_a (vertical 1080x1920, 36.45 s)

Source: Rabbi Avshalom Schneider, lesson before Tisha b'Av 5785 (kolhalashon 41852449), 1930.62-1970.06 with 2 cuts. Voice mixed locally.
Idea: Tisha b'Av is the one fully "Haredi" festival. Every other festival was successfully distorted and reinvented (Pesach and the rest
became new inventions, an "agriculture festival", every festival got some variation). But Tisha b'Av cannot be distorted - it is ours;
only we are meant to feel it. It is exactly the opposite of what they want: they say "no more Tisha b'Av", we insist "there IS Tisha b'Av" -
we are in exile, it hurts, and everything that is here is nothing.
Central visual metaphor: a REPAINTING CONVEYOR. Festival objects ride a conveyor belt through a coral "repaint arch" machine and come out
changed (garish, toy-like). Off the belt, on the floor, sits a low dark STONE with ONE small candle flame (Tisha b'Av) and a black brimmed
hat resting beside it ("Haredi"). The machine tries to reach the stone and fails. A glossy cover tries to cover it and is pushed off.
In the end everything shiny dissolves to nothing; the stone, the flame and far broken column stumps remain.
