## b1_bathtub  (t0 0.0, 44.9 s, 5 cues) - the bathtub metaphor
- 0-5.5 title window: WS of a bright cartoon bathroom-like stage (tiled wall, window with a sky view, rug) with BT in the centre, filling half the frame; G sits on the rim, T in the tub up to his shoulders, floating.
- c1 (5.5-12.1) MS, slight low angle: BT appears and fills (tap runs, water rises), the white level ruler on the tub side shows a red mark at the water line; T bobs and hums (happy), G watches. Fg: a rubber-duck-sized blurred sponge/soap at the edge.
- c2 (12.7-19.2) INS tap + cloud: the tap above the tub turns off (valve handle rotates, drips stop; sfx tick); a small rain cloud (cloud with rain) above fades away. G reaches and turns the valve (arm), T looks up at the empty tap.
- c3 (19.8-27.7) MS: a 3D calendar flips pages fast (12 flips = one year) beside the tub; a red question mark pops over it; T's face goes from happy to curious, G taps the calendar.
- c4 (28.3-35.6) CU on the water line: the line sinks slowly along the ruler; a bracket between old and new line (overlay "level drop"); T slides down with the water, sitting lower in the tub, looking at G.
- c5 (36.2-42.7) WS: the empty space above the water blinks; the tap and cloud return as glowing outlines each with a question mark; G and T both look up at them (match look), T raises hands.

## b2_from_hermon  (t0 44.9, 42.7 s, 6 cues) - Tipa's journey starts
- c1 (0.9-6.8) WS high over LM north (bounds Hermon to Kinneret south end): Hermon with snow cap (SH), cloud-with-snow, flakes falling; T sits ON the snow (Tipa as a drop of snow-water, white-blue tint), G stands on the Golan edge pointing up at the peak.
- c2 (7.4-13.3) MCU on T on the slope: flakes turn into drops, T melts, sinks into the ground (dotted line down through a rock cutaway) and comes out lower at the three spring mouths as three small blue dots (T emerges from the Dan mouth).
- c3 (13.9-20.4) three streams light up one by one (Dan, Banias, Hatzbani overlays 90 px): camera glides along each; T (small) rides a stream on his back, G points in turn.
- c4 (21.0-27.6) the three lines meet in the Hula valley (blue wet patch), one thick blue line = the Jordan; WS from the east, crane down; T floats down the line.
- c5 (28.2-34.1) the line flows south (animated dash shader) and pours into the Kinneret from the north; the lake darkens a little; T drops in with a splash and bobs; G watches from the west shore.
- c6 (34.7-40.5) pull back to a huge blue arrow from Hermon to the Kinneret over the whole map, the word "Jordan" at 90 px (overlay); T on the arrow's tip, arms:'up' cheer; G nods.
