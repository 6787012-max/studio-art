# STORYBOARD - khs_85 "the one inside the cocoon cannot imagine the meadow" (vertical, ~51 s, objects only, no people)

Central metaphor: a striped silk COCOON (egg) with a glowing seed inside; outside it a vast spring meadow the inside never sees; the seed becomes a butterfly = a completely different form of life. Camera: starts inside, slow push-ins, then a long reveal dolly out through the opened cap and up into white-gold sky.

| time (s) | idea | object that acts it out | what the viewer learns |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-11 | when redemption comes we will not believe how we were satisfied | warm striped chamber, glowing seed on a cushion, hazy window cap; seed pulses and a ring expands on "will not believe" | we sit content in a closed world |
| 11-22 | parable of the fetus; birth = redemption | seed heartbeat pulses with rings on each "birth" | being born is the coming change |
| 22-28 | good for him there, best days, learns Torah with the angel, Gan Eden | rays, floating petals, scroll (no letters) + star of light descends, petals burst up | a perfect cozy world |
| 28-34 | cannot imagine any other form of life outside | milky cap, blurred green silhouette of a butterfly crossing outside, seed oblivious | the outside exists but is not in his picture |
| 34-45 | we too are in such a pregnancy: let us renovate, improve, make it stronger and more | brush, roller, trowel; colour stripes pop on the wall, pillows pop, extra layer, cap gets thicker | an improved cocoon is still a cocoon |
| 45-51 | completely different, another world, spirituality, nothing else | cap opens, flash, seed becomes butterfly, camera dollies out into meadow and sky, butterfly rises into white-gold light | not an upgrade - a different life |
