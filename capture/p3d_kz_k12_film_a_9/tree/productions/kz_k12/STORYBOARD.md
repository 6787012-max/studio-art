# kz_k12 STORYBOARD - "the YESH we stop seeing" (Rabbi Avshalom Schneider, kol halashon lesson 40298587)

Central visual metaphor: a large pair of round wooden-framed GLASSES floats in front of the world. Desire fogs the lenses
(soft grey-pink haze), and the abundant "yesh" behind them (things we already have) fades to pale outlines. Contemplation and
saying thanks wipe the lenses clear, and the objects glow in full colour again. In the second half the same glasses look at the
community: tiny lit houses become whole cities that grew against all odds (Purim: life given without merit).

Characters: NONE in the whole film. People are represented by objects (slippers/shoes, small lanterns, lit windows).
Palette (different from k06 desert, k07 green, k08 terracotta, k10 indigo, k11 walnut/plum): early-spring Adar -
fresh sage/mint #9CCBA8, soft coral #F28C6B, cream #FFF4E0, light sky blue #A8D8F0, accent honey #F5B84A (spice only),
ink outline #3A2E28. Flat toon materials, clean empty backgrounds, bright, never dark/black.
