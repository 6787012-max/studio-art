Lesson idea (English paraphrase, the real voice is mixed locally): the author of the Tanya explains why the Torah is full of laws about
strange cases that never happened and never will: because it is God's will that in such a case this is the law. Whoever understands
such a law cleaves to His will, piece after piece; one who knows the whole Torah is attached with no point left out.

Central metaphor: a big SPHERE OF LIGHT (His will) hanging under a soft sun-orb, built from hexagonal "law tiles". Odd-shaped coral
tiles (the strange cases) never land on the small globe (the practical world) - they lock into the sphere instead. At the end the sphere is complete.

Character: none (objects only). Palette: navy 0x1f3a5a, teal 0x3f8f8a, gold 0xf2c14e, coral 0xe8825a, cream 0xf6efe0 on a bright pale teal-cream sky.
