# sod_hazman_v4 - direction for chapters c2..c10 (supervisor, 01/11/2026 night)

Same rules as BRIEF_c1.md (read it first: zero Hebrew, no people, deterministic, timeline-driven, toon+ink, warm light).
Reuse the c1 modules unchanged: `lib_v4/caption3d.js`, `lib_v4/watch.js` (and anything else c1 put in lib_v4/).
The line-by-line picture is the `visual` field of each line in `timeline.json`; this file adds sets, look and rules.
Every chapter: 4 s title card (vault `T_C<n>`), then lines; `dwell` after a line = time for the animation to finish
showing the idea (keep it moving: the explained object must animate during dwell, never a frozen frame).

## Visual language (all chapters)
- One "explainer object" per idea, big and centered (Earth, day bar, clock, orbit, calendar ring, sefer), shot in 3D with
  camera moves; labels/arrows as 3D panels; numbers count up; diagrams build step by step in sync with the words.
- Cuts every 3-6 s inside long lines (size changes WS/MS/CU/insert); a camera move on every shot with easing.
- The watch appears in every W line (MCU, 45-60% frame height) and reacts in small insets elsewhere.
- Chapter end hook line: dramatic push-in + light flicker + a glimpse of the next chapter's main object.
- Q cards: the scene behind slows to a calm loop of the chapter's main object; the 60 s ring counts.
