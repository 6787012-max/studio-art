# STORYBOARD - khs_81 "One grain every thousand years" (vertical, 66.74 s)

Central metaphor: a MOUNTAIN OF GOLDEN GRAINS on an endless warm plain, and a small bird that takes ONE grain every thousand years. Something unimaginably big is still FINITE - it will end. Against it: a beam of pure light that has NO end (the camera can follow it and never reach its top). His wisdom is not "a million times ours" - it is not on the same scale at all.
