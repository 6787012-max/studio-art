# kz_k14 STORYBOARD - "there is no dead time" (Rabbi Avshalom Schneider, kol halashon lesson 42772338)

Continues chapter k05 ("the point inside the letter bet"). Theme: boredom / the existential void, and the secret of turning the
empty time itself towards the King.

Central visual metaphor: the EMPTY GAP in the DAY RIBBON. A long day ribbon (24 equal segment tiles, like a calendar strip) runs
through the scenes; boredom = a grey hollow gap of 2 tiles (a wire-frame empty box). The world throws colourful ENTERTAINMENT boxes
into the gap - they fall through and the gap stays empty. Our way: the gap itself tilts up towards a warm light from above (the King,
shown as a simple golden CROWN of light high above), and then opens like a box: many small TASK objects come out of it. At the end
every tile of the ribbon glows - no dead time. Second recurring hero: the BET-HOUSE - a 3D shape like an open-front house (roof bar,
back wall, floor bar extending forward, built from boxes; it echoes the letter bet but it is a shape, not text) with a glowing DOT
(the dagesh) inside = each person's own point.

Characters: NONE in the whole film. People are implied only by objects (a tray, a small cozy house with a lit window, a cup).
Palette (different from k13 slate/terracotta, k12 sage/coral, k11 walnut/plum, k10 indigo): soft teal #3E8E8A, peach #F2B48C,
butter cream #FFF4E0, sky mint #CFE8E4, sunflower #F2C14E (spice only: the dot, the crown light), ink outline #24414A.
Grey for the empty gap: warm grey #A9A39A (never black). Flat toon, bright, clean.

Times are SOURCE seconds (mapped to clip time in shots_k14.json). No Hebrew/Latin text on any prop; digits allowed.
