# khs_95 storyboard - short95 "tfila acheret legamrei" (phase B, film_a, 40.24 s)

Idea: life is meant to advance; one who keeps contemplating gains new perception and does not stay at bar-mitzva age. Someone who prays at 13 and at 70 the same prayer stays a little child; the prayer must grow with his view of creation. At bar mitzva one prays because everyone prays; prayer should become part of life, because one lives Hashem more and is more connected to Him.

Look: dusty-rose morning sky to cream horizon, terracotta floor, ochre hill face, olive growth, burgundy prayer books with cream pages, soft gold light. Side-view lateral tracking dolly, no orbit, no vertical crane. Objects only: no people, hands, faces or silhouettes. In-scene Hebrew comes from the vault labels LBL_A to LBL_G.
