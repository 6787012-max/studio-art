# STORYBOARD - khs_80 / film_a "you cannot want to want" (vertical, 35.12 s)

Central metaphor: a small cream PAPER BOAT on a winding teal RIVER (= a person's life path). Inside the boat floats a small golden ORB (= the person's real will). We cannot catch or create that orb ourselves; what really moves the boat at the big forks is a soft warm CURRENT / light from above (= Hashem's will). Looking back from high above, every turn of the river is lit by threads from ONE warm light source.
