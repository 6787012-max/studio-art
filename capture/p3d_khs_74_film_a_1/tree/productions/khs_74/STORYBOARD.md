# STORYBOARD - khs_74 (vertical short, 56.97 s voice + 0.4 s still tail = 57.37 s)

Lesson idea (English paraphrase, the real voice is mixed locally; Daat Tevunot lesson): there is no "strict law" (din) standing alone.
Din was CREATED by the attribute of love. All of nature is din: fixed rules that always work, no exceptions (a screwdriver in a socket
explodes, a dropped glass breaks, a knife cuts wood; mercy does not step in). Who made these rules? Love - so that there will be order.
The spiritual laws God engraved work the same way: if a person does not keep Torah and mitzvot, it does not work - they are spiritual laws of nature.

Central metaphor: a warm ROSE-GOLD LOVE ORB that FORGES a cool STEEL-BLUE GRID OF RULES. The grid is the floor the whole little world stands on
(tree, rock, socket, glass, log). At the end the orb is revealed as the source above the grid, and a second, golden grid (spiritual laws)
appears above it, wired to the orb; a lantern on it stays dark where the golden cable has a gap.

Character: none (objects only). Location: a big wooden WORKBENCH world on a warm sand floor (different from khs_73: no book, no tiles, no sky-sphere).
Palette: cream/sand sky, steel-blue 0x4a6f8f (din), rose-gold 0xe59a7a + warm gold 0xf2b84e (love), sage 0x8fae7e, walnut 0x7a5236.
Camera style: lateral dolly along the bench + one top-down shot + a final crane up (khs_73 used orbit/crane around a stand).
