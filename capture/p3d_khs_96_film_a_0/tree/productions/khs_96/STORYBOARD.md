# khs_96 storyboard - short96 "kelev bechagora" (the leashed dog), phase B, film_a, 32.98 s

Idea: evil had to get real power. Created from the start inside a cage it would be limited, a tame dog with no power to tempt you. We want it free; then it is caught on a leash and still rages and wants the whole world. That is why it was done in two stages: a dog in a cage does not excite you, but a free dog now on a leash barks.

Look: butter-yellow sky fading to cream; fresh spring-green meadow; caramel toon dog with a cream muzzle; navy-iron cage; tomato-red collar and leash; light-oak post and signs. Two stations on the meadow (cage left, post right), a toy village past the post, a toy thermometer. Side moves only: low tracking push-ins, one lateral pan between stations, a final rise and pull-back. No orbit, no crane, the camera never enters the cage.

Objects only: no people, hands, arms, faces or silhouettes. The one animal is the same toon dog in every shot. In-scene Hebrew comes from the vault labels LBL_A to LBL_H only.
