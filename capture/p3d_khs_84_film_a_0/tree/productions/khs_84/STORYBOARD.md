# STORYBOARD - khs_84 "the mother he never saw / what to long for" (vertical, ~65 s, objects only, no people)

Central metaphor: an EMPTY picture frame at the far end of a long table = the mother he never knew. Slow crane: high overhead descending to table level, then push-ins.

| time (s) | idea | object that acts it out | what the viewer learns |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-10 | every year his birthday is her yahrzeit; years pass | yahrzeit glass candle + birthday cake candle both lit, calendar pages flip, light sweeps | birthday and yahrzeit are the same day |
| 10-21 | flame passes to the cake candle; the family waits for his bar mitzvah kaddish | spark travels yahrzeit -> cake; siddur opens with warm glow | the waiting for the kaddish |
| 21-31 | he says it flat; anger and tears | flat grey blocks rise from siddur; tea glass trembles, drops fall | cold recital, family hurt |
| 31-41 | tension breaks; the empty frame arrives | blocks fall; frame rises from the table | the one he never saw |
| 41-47 | no pictures, no bond | thread from siddur reaches toward the frame and stops short | no connection |
| 47-56 | she never hugged or smiled at me | empty cradle with folded blanket, cold light | never held |
| 56-65 | lesson: we do not know what to long for | frame becomes a window onto far golden Mikdash; small dim house near; thread stops short; frame glows | we never knew the Shechina below |
